# Middle Ground — Support

Need a hand? Email **support@middleground.app** and we'll get back to you.

When you write in, it helps to include your iOS version, your device, and what you were doing
when it went wrong.

## Common questions

### How do I connect with someone?
When you set up Middle Ground you either **invite someone** or **join with a code**.

- If you invited: you'll see a 6-character code on the final setup screen, and again in
  **Profile → Invite** until someone joins. Share it however you like.
- If you're joining: choose **I have a code** during setup and type the code you were given.
  Codes ignore spaces, dashes, and capitalisation.

Codes avoid look-alike characters (no `O`/`0`, no `I`/`1`/`L`), so they're safe to read aloud.

### I can't send a request
Requests need someone to send them *to*. Until a second person joins your group, the compose
screen will say so. Share your invite code from **Profile → Invite**.

### My partner isn't getting notifications
Check that notifications are enabled in **Profile → Push Notifications**, and in iOS Settings →
Notifications → Middle Ground. Notifications are best-effort and can be delayed — the request
itself always appears in the app.

### Can I respond to a request I sent?
No, by design. The person you sent it to decides. If you sent something by mistake, open it and
choose **Cancel request**.

### What do the response types mean?
- **Accept** — you're in.
- **Decline** — not this time.
- **Negotiate** — you're open to it, but let's talk.
- **Counter** — reply with a specific alternative.
- **Reschedule** — same idea, different time.
- **Save** — keep it for later.

### How do streaks and XP work?
You earn XP whenever you respond to a request, and your streak grows on each day you respond.
Responding twice in one day still counts as one day. Progress is stored on your device.

### How do I delete my account?
**Profile → Delete Account**, then confirm. This is permanent: your account, your progress, and
your requests are removed. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) for exactly what's erased.

### Where's my data stored?
On Google Firebase, and readable only by you and the person you paired with — enforced on the
server, not just in the app. Your XP and streak never leave your device. Full detail is in the
[Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).

## Reporting a security issue

Please email **support@middleground.app** with "Security" in the subject rather than filing it
publicly. We'll acknowledge and work with you on a fix.
