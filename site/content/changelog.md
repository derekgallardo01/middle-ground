# What's new

Everything we've shipped, newest first. Currently on version 1.0, in App Store review.

Screenshots show the app as it looks today.

<div class="release current">
<div class="release-head"><span class="release-version">2 August 2026</span> <span class="tag">Latest</span></div>
<div class="with-shot">
<ul>
<li><strong>Groups can hold more than two people.</strong> Until now a group was a pair, full stop. Now a plan can go to several people at once — and if one of them can't make it, the night still goes ahead for everyone else.</li>
<li><strong>You can call off a plan you'd already agreed to.</strong> Before, once someone said yes, the plan couldn't be cancelled at all. If you cancel close to the time, the app tells you it counts as a late cancellation — before you do it, not weeks later.</li>
<li><strong>The other person hears "at short notice"</strong> when a plan is called off near the time, because that's the part people actually mind.</li>
<li><strong>The app is faster.</strong> Several screens were quietly loading the same thing up to six times over; now they load it once. Opening the app no longer waits on the network to decide whether you're signed in.</li>
<li><strong>Everything moves the same way.</strong> Animations were inconsistent across screens and some ignored the iPhone's "Reduce Motion" setting entirely. One system now, and it respects that setting everywhere.</li>
<li>Replies in a conversation now appear as they arrive, instead of just being there.</li>
<li>Fixed a Cancel button that was offered on plans it couldn't actually cancel — tapping it just returned an error. Found on a real phone, not in the simulator.</li>
<li>Deleting your account now also removes your notification preferences, which were being left behind.</li>
</ul>

</div>
</div>

<div class="release">
<div class="release-head"><span class="release-version">1 August 2026</span></div>
<div class="with-shot">
<ul>
<li><strong>You choose what you're notified about.</strong> Five kinds of alert, each with its own switch. Nothing is turned on behind your back.</li>
<li><strong>The app asks if a plan happened.</strong> A few hours afterwards, so you don't have to remember to tell it.</li>
<li><strong>You'll know when a plan is cancelled, and why.</strong> Before, plans just quietly vanished.</li>
<li><strong>Share where you are.</strong> Around the time of a plan, with one tap. It's deleted when the plan's over.</li>
<li><strong>Put something on it.</strong> Both of you can stake points on a plan happening. Turn up and you both get them back.</li>
<li><strong>See who actually shows up.</strong> Built from plans that happened, not opinions. Partners can't see each other's.</li>
<li><strong>Invite someone to a single plan.</strong> A code that gets them into that plan only — not your group, and nothing after it.</li>
<li><strong>Suggested places</strong> when you say where you're meeting, instead of typing it out.</li>
<li><strong>A weekly nudge</strong> if you haven't planned anything with someone in a while. Once a week at most, and never when you've already got something booked.</li>
<li>Suggesting a new time now works properly — say "Sunday instead?" and if they agree, the plan moves.</li>
<li>Fixed the number on the app icon, which was missing plans waiting on you.</li>
<li>Fixed screens flashing empty before loading, and rebuilt the plan screen, which was cramped.</li>
</ul>
