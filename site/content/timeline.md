# Timeline

Where the app has got to, and what's coming. The [changelog](/changelog) has the detail.

## So far

<ul class="timeline">
<li class="done"><span class="when">18 July 2026</span><span class="what">First version people could use</span><p>Make a plan, agree a time, done.</p></li>
<li class="done"><span class="when">26 July 2026</span><span class="what">Private testing opens</span><p>Real people, real plans — which is where the assumptions started meeting reality.</p></li>
<li class="done"><span class="when">30 July 2026</span><span class="what">Groups and quick plans</span><p>More than one group per person, each with its own invite code, plus one-tap plans for the things people ask constantly.</p></li>
<li class="done"><span class="when">31 July 2026</span><span class="what">Submitted to the App Store</span><p>Along with the feature everything else depends on: the app asking, afterwards, whether a plan actually happened.</p></li>
<li class="done"><span class="when">1 August 2026</span><span class="what">Following through</span><p>Points staked on a plan happening. Notifications you control. Location that disappears when the plan is over.</p></li>
<li class="now"><span class="when">Now</span><span class="what">Waiting on Apple</span><p>Version 1.0 is in review. We're still shipping to testers in the meantime.</p></li>

## What's next

<ul class="timeline">
<li class="now"><span class="when">In progress</span><span class="what">Groups of three or more</span><p>Right now a group is two people. Three changes what a plan means — if one person can't make it, the night should still go ahead for everyone else.</p></li>
<li><span class="when">Next</span><span class="what">A friendly scoreboard</span><p>Who shows up most, within a group of friends. Not between couples — that one stays off.</p></li>
<li><span class="when">Next</span><span class="what">Cancelling at the last minute</span><p>Calling something off an hour before isn't the same as calling it off on Tuesday. The app should know the difference without being harsh about it.</p></li>
<li><span class="when">Exploring</span><span class="what">Places worth going</span><p>Suggested places are in. Booking a table, and working with restaurants directly, needs deals rather than software.</p></li>
<li><span class="when">Exploring</span><span class="what">When people disagree</span><p>Two people can remember the same evening differently. Today nobody is penalised when that happens. Going further means someone has to review appeals, which means paying a person to do it.</p></li>
</ul>

We ship most days, and everything above went out in under two weeks.
